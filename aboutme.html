About me

Hi, I'm Luca Raets, a student at Fontys ICT, where I spend my time creating digital products and bringing ideas to life through code. At school, I work on a variety of ICT projects, including games like Crusaders Quest and Blackjack, as well as the website you're reading right now. What I love about the field of ICT is how fast it evolves and how it gives you the freedom to build anything you can imagine. It's a place where creativity meets problem-solving, and every project is a new opportunity to learn, innovate, and make something meaningful.
